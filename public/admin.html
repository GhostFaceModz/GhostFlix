2Import

Paste a YouTube playlist/channel URL, or an Internet Archive collection or search URL. GhostFlix fetches every video, matches it to TMDB by title + year, and lets you review before saving.

3Link patterns (your own host)

Titles with no specific source automatically use these. Placeholders: {tmdb_id}, and for series {season} + {episode}. Any host is allowed.

4Live TV channels

Add free, legal live channels (official streams only — e.g. a broadcaster’s YouTube live URL youtube.com/channel/UC…/live or their official HLS). Curated defaults live in data/live-channels.json.
